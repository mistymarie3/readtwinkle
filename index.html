About the Book

Twinkle is a little star with a big dream. All around, the other stars keep growing bigger and brighter, but Twinkle stays very small.

So Twinkle tries everything: stretching, spinning, and squeezing with all its might, until something unexpected happens that changes everything.

A sparkly story about trying, trying again, and discovering that you don’t have to be like everyone else to shine. Includes activity pages to help young readers find their own special spark.

“What makes you different may be exactly what makes you shine.”

About the Author

Misty Young is a writer, actress, model, dancer, and dreamer who knows what it feels like to wonder whether you belong.

When the idea for Twinkle came to her, Misty realized it was more than a story about a little star. It was also about learning that you do not have to be like everyone else to shine.

Twinkle was written for every child who has ever wondered, “Can I do that too?” Misty hopes this story reminds readers to try, keep trying, and discover what can happen when they believe in who they already are.

Keep trying. Keep moving forward. Your purpose is yours to discover.

For my nieces and nephews, may you always know that what makes you different may be exactly what makes you shine.With all my love, Auntie Misty ♡
